Solutions

Engagements that pay for themselves.

Whether you need a fast diagnostic or a full network transformation, every engagement is scoped to prove value early and build capability that stays.

A

Factory Diagnostic

A rapid, data-driven assessment of a plant or line: OEE loss analysis, downtime and yield mapping, and a prioritized opportunity model. You leave with a quantified business case and a clear first move — typically in 2–4 weeks.

B

AIM Deployment

Instrument the plant, connect existing MES/PLC data, and stand up real-time OEE, predictive maintenance and anomaly detection. We pilot on your highest-loss lines, prove the numbers, then scale.

SEM Rollout

Install the human operating system: goal cascade, daily management rituals, visual performance boards and frontline coaching — so the insight from AIM turns into action on every shift.

D

Factory of the Future — HPO

The full High-Performance Organization program: AIM and SEM deployed together across a plant or network, with capability transfer designed to make your team self-sufficient.
