About

Four decades on the shop floor, distilled.

PacersGlobal exists to close the gap between what modern manufacturing can do and what most plants actually do — combining hard-won operational discipline with the newest generation of AI.

Madhav built and ran high-performing plants long before "Industry 4.0" had a name — mastering the operating disciplines that separate world-class factories from the rest. He founded PacersGlobal to bring that playbook, now amplified by AI, to manufacturers ready to modernize.
